## Weeks 2–4: paid ads (optional, small budget)

### Facebook/Instagram "Click to WhatsApp" ads (best fit: every click becomes a chat)
In **Meta Ads Manager**: objective **Engagement → Messaging apps → WhatsApp**.
- Location: **Batala**, radius ~10 km around Dera Road. Age 25–65. Languages: leave open.
- Budget: start **₹150–250 per day** for 14 days.
- Ads: image `2-offer-pa` / `3-prescription-pa` (and the story versions), caption from the table, button **Send WhatsApp message**.
- Pre-filled message: "Hello Shanker Medicos, I saw your ad."
- Watch **Cost per messaging conversation** in Ads Manager; keep the ad that is cheapest, pause the other.

### Google (optional)
Google Business Profile does most of the work for free. If you want ads, use **Google Ads → Search**, location Batala, ₹150/day, keywords: *medical store batala, chemist batala, pharmacy batala, medical shop dera road batala*.

### Rules for medicine ads (to avoid rejection or legal trouble)
- Never name or show **prescription medicines** in ads, and never claim an ad cures or treats anything (Drugs and Magic Remedies Act; Meta and Google health policies).
- Advertise the **shop**: WhatsApp ordering, the offer on *selected medicines*, opening hours, location. These are the images in this kit.
- Meta and Google restrict ads for *online* medicine sales. You sell in the shop, so say "order on WhatsApp, collect at the shop" (don't promise delivery in ads).
- WhatsApp broadcasts only to customers who saved your number and agreed; stop for anyone who says STOP.
