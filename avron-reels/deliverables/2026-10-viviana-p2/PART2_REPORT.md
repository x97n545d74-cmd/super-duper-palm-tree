# Viviana Power Tech, Part 2: who pays, what they build, where the money goes
Researched 24 Oct 2026. **P** = primary filing or transcript, **S** = secondary. Education only. No buy/sell/hold view.

## 1. Government or private clients?
**Every announced order is from a Government of Gujarat utility.** These are all six orders announced to NSE from Mar to Oct 2026 [P, NSE filings]:

| Date | Client | Value (incl. taxes) | Work |
|---|---|---|---|
| 31 Mar 2026 | PGVCL | ₹50.61 cr | 11 kV covered conductor (MVCC) and lattice poles on coastal feeders (Bhuj, Bhavnagar, Jamnagar) under the *Cyclone Resilient Network* of the Viksit Gujarat Scheme |
| 7 Jul 2026 | DGVCL | ₹41.50 cr | 11 kV underground cable under the "Overhead to Underground" scheme |
| 9 Jul 2026 | PGVCL | ₹71.39 cr | Conversion of 11 kV HT and LT lines to an underground cable network with a ring main system |
| 22 Jul 2026 | PGVCL | ₹128.35 cr | 11 kV MVCC in the Bhuj and Rajkot Rural circles, System Improvement scheme |
| 27 Aug 2026 | GETCO | ₹9.74 cr | 66 kV XLPE cable laying |
| 10 Oct 2026 | MGVCL | ₹466.74 cr | 11 kV open line → MVCC and underground network, "SI Scheme – Robust-III" (the filing says it was already counted as L1) |
| **Total** | | **₹768.33 cr** | |

- **Who owns these clients.** MGVCL, PGVCL and DGVCL are distribution companies, and GETCO is the transmission company. All four are subsidiaries of GUVNL, which is **wholly owned by the Government of Gujarat** [S: GUVNL/Wikipedia, CARE/ICRA rating notes].
- **Private clients.** Management said in Dec 2025 that it has relationships with "state utilities as well as private utilities" [P, 4 Dec 2025 call]. **I found no announced private order.**
- **Battery-storage projects.** Both are government-linked: Gujarat (GUVNL) and Rajasthan [P, both calls].
- **Where the money comes from.** In Aug 2026 management said: "we are executing the projects mainly backed by the funds backed by Central Government" [P, Q1 FY27 call]. In Dec 2025: "60% of the payment is made by the Central Government" [P].

### Why government (AVRON interpretation, labelled as such)
- **Who runs distribution.** In most of India, power distribution is run by state-owned discoms. Grid upgrades are paid for by schemes, from the Central Government's RDSS and from Gujarat's own Viksit Gujarat and SI schemes.
- **Why Gujarat is spending on this.** Gujarat's coastal feeders face cyclones. These orders replace bare overhead lines with covered conductors and underground cable.
- **How government EPC works:**
  - Contracts go to the lowest bidder (L1).
  - Payment comes after milestones and inspection.
  - Spending bunches up at the financial year-end.
  - This fits Part 1's pattern of **61% of FY26 revenue in Q4 and 267 debtor days**. That link is our interpretation; the company says no client pays late.

## 2. What is the product?
1. **Power T&D EPC (the core business).** 11–66 kV underground cabling, covered conductors and substations, up to 400 kV. Management: "our main core business is power transmission and distribution… for at least a decade" [P, Dec 2025].
2. **Transformers (new).**
   - **Factory inaugurated 21 Sep 2026** at Por, Vadodara: power transformers **up to 20 MVA, 66 kV and 33 kV classes, up to 1,000 MVA a year** [P, NSE filing].
   - **Greenfield 132 kV plant (phase 1).** About **₹90 cr capex, ₹3.5–4 cr spent** by Aug 2026, about ₹10 cr planned for FY27 [P, Q1 call]. Targets: 132 kV and 63 MVA next, then 220 and 400 kV.
   - **Aarsh Transformers** (distribution transformers up to 500 kVA) was sold to a non-promoter buyer.
3. **Battery energy storage (BESS)** [P, Dec 2025 call]:
   - **Order book.** About **₹500 cr** of the order book (two projects: Gujarat and Rajasthan).
   - **How it works.** This isn't EPC revenue. Viviana **invests** about **₹300 cr per project**, 75–80% of it borrowed, with about **₹59 cr of subsidy**. It earns about **₹40 cr+ a year for 10–12 years** after an 18-month build.
   - **Status (Aug 2026).** Gujarat is waiting for GUVNL's notice to proceed. Rajasthan has its land and is waiting for approval [P, Q1 call].
4. **Data centres:** "our team is working out, studying on that" [P, Dec 2025]. Nothing concrete yet.

## 3. Where some of the money went: related party (new in Part 2)
From the **12th AGM notice**, 29 Sep 2026 [P, NSE]:
- **Advances paid.** Viviana paid **₹22.55 cr in FY26** and **₹13.53 cr from 1 Apr to 3 Sep 2026** to **Viviana Life Spaces Pvt Ltd** as "advance against capex". That's **₹36.08 cr in total**.
- **Who owns it now.** In Aug 2026 Viviana sold its 90% stake in that company to **promoter Mrs. Priyanka Richi Choksi** [P, 4 Aug outcome; completion reported 17 Aug].
- **Its size.** Viviana Life Spaces' FY26 **turnover was ₹1.05 lakh**, its PAT ₹143.26 lakh and its net worth ₹480.01 lakh [P, AGM notice].
- **New approval sought.** The AGM sought approval for an **advance of up to ₹50 cr** "towards the purchase of office premises". The notice says this is 5% of consolidated turnover and "approximately 47,61,904% of the related party's annual standalone turnover".
- **Management on collateral** [P, Q1 call]: the company will get "collateral of around INR100 crores by 2030 from Viviana Life Spaces… for bank facilities". After the sale it says "the promoters are same… we will able to utilize the securities properties as a collateral".
- **Not verified:** the AGM voting result for this resolution.

## 4. What future the company talks about (management targets, not AVRON forecasts)
- **FY27 revenue target:** ₹875–910 cr. **Q1 FY27 was ₹72 cr**, so the remaining three quarters need about **₹270 cr each** (AVRON calc.).
- **FY30 target:** ₹2,000–2,200 cr of revenue and ₹200 cr+ of PAT. Of that, transformers are meant to contribute ₹400–500 cr [P, Q1 call].
- **Bigger bids.** Ambition to bid ₹1,000–2,000 cr TBCB projects "within couple of years". New states being bid: Haryana, Rajasthan, Bihar and Uttarakhand.
- **Pipeline.** Unexecuted orders plus L1 positions total about ₹840 cr. Bids worth more than ₹1,400 cr are under evaluation and more than ₹2,600 cr are being bid.
- **Margin guide.** Management said "nearly 9%–9.5% annually". The context suggests PAT margin.
- **Funding.** In Aug, management said "development is going on, including the investment from the promoters". The board then dropped the warrant issue on 15 Oct.
  - The board first met on **10 Oct, 6:30–8:15 pm**, and deferred the decision. The 22:48 notice called the 15 Oct meeting [S: Kalkine summary of the 10 Oct filing].
