# Palantir reel 1: caption (paste into Instagram)

In May 2024 the US Army gave Palantir a $480 million contract for its Maven software. In May 2025 it added $795 million, taking the total to about $1.28 billion (≈ ₹11,000 crore).

Maven uses AI to read drone and satellite images. Palantir sells it as user licences.

Profit: in 2025 Lockheed Martin's operating margin was about 10%. Palantir's was 32% (GAAP).
