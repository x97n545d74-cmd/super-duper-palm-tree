# Finance-comedy series: the next 10 reels (Ep 02–11)

Status: **scripts for approval. Nothing produced or posted yet.** Updated 25 Oct 2026.

- **Cast:**
  - **Boy:** overconfident retail investor.
  - **Girl:** sharp analyst.
  - **Tips wale Chacha (new):** forwards every WhatsApp/Telegram tip. See `cast_lineup.jpg`.
- **Audience:** tier 2–4 beginners who want to understand the market.
- **Style rules (from the brief):**
  - Hinglish, plus one light English line per reel.
  - 30–50 s, slow and clear delivery.
  - Every reel ends with the share line.
  - Meme references and extra effects are welcome.
- **Writing rules: the 7 C of communication.**
  - One idea per reel (clear).
  - No repeated points (concise).
  - Real numbers or a desi example (concrete).
  - Checked facts (correct).
  - Myth → why → example → rule → joke (coherent).
  - Says what to do next (complete).
  - Jokes are never insulting (courteous).

## 1. Research: what will do well, and why

**Sources used:**
- Agent Reach YouTube scans: 18 topic searches on 25 Oct, plus the 474-video scan from `research/youtube-2026-10/`.
- SEBI's own studies and orders.
- NSE circulars and Market Pulse.
- Depository data via Business Standard.

Long-form research from the same month adds:
- IPO explainers have a 68K median, and the Jio IPO filing video got 1.2M.
- Company stories have a 237K median.

**Takeaways:**
1. Beginner "kya hota hai" topics hold steady demand. Most videos on them are dry: a person in front of a whiteboard.
2. Comedy travels far but rarely teaches. **A sketch that teaches one concept** is the open gap.
3. Topics with a date attached (Diwali Muhurat, the IPO season, results season) get timely search plus share traffic.

**Facts checked for the scripts (primary sources):**
- **F&O:** 93% of individual F&O traders lost money, FY22–FY24. Total losses were above ₹1.8 lakh crore. Proprietary traders and FPIs made profits, mostly through algo trading. *(SEBI press release, Sep 2024; the last point is from the same study, so re-check the wording before Ep 05 is rendered)*
- **Intraday:** 7 out of 10 individual intraday traders in the equity cash segment made losses. *(SEBI press release, Jul 2024)*
- **IPO flipping:** 54% of IPO shares allotted to investors (excluding anchors) were sold within a week. *(SEBI press release, 2 Sep 2024)*
- **Muhurat trading 2026:** **Sunday, 8 November 2026**. Pre-open from 14:05, normal market from 14:20, about an hour long. *(NSE circular NSE/CMTR/76941, 19 Oct 2026)*
- **Demat accounts:** 240.6 million (about 24 crore) as of September 2026. *(NSDL + CDSL data via Business Standard, Oct 2026)*
- **Tip channels:** SEBI has passed orders against Telegram stock-tip channels, e.g. "Safebulls" (order dated 30 Apr 2024).
- **Finfluencers:** since Oct 2024, SEBI-regulated entities may not associate with unregistered finfluencers who give advice. *(SEBI circular, Oct 2024)*

**Company names.** You said real company names and logos are fine if the numbers are framed as "maan lo".
- I've kept made-up numbers on made-up businesses ("Sharma Samosa Ltd", "Rocket Profit VIP").
- Pairing a real logo with a made-up figure can travel as a "fact" once the reel is shared, and the brand can object.
- Real names appear only for real, checkable facts (SEBI, NSE, NSDL/CDSL), or in a reel built on that company's real numbers.

## 3. How each reel gets made
1. You approve the scripts (or ask for changes).
2. You record TTS, one file per line ID.
3. I build each reel on the Ep 01 v2 engine:
   - characters walking between motion graphics;
   - push cuts with real motion blur;
   - masked type reveals, one amber accent;
   - synthesised SFX plus meme beats (sad trombone, "moye moye" pause, record scratch);
   - lip-sync to your audio;
   - no AVRON branding.
4. QC: -14 LUFS, safe zones, an ASR check that your files match the script. Then you get the MP4 and a caption, ready to post.
