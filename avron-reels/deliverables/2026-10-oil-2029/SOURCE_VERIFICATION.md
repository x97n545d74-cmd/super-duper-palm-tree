## Corrections made during fact-check
1. **The shock clause** was first attributed (via a search summary) to the 2026-27 PIB release. It doesn't appear in the 2026-27 speech or the PIB summary. It is in the **Budget 2025-26 FRBM statement**, and the on-screen citation was corrected to that document.
2. **RBI "first since 2023"** was made specific: "February 2023", attributed to Business Standard as cross-checked secondary reporting.
3. **The debt chart** plots only the two official figures (56.1 RE, 55.6 BE). The path to 50±1 is drawn dashed and labelled "glide path, not data".

## Interpretation (labelled on screen)
- "Bill भरा जाएगा: Company, सरकार या आप" is AVRON's framing and is tagged **AVRON INTERPRETATION**.
- The film does **not** claim the target has been dropped, that the oil shock legally triggers the clause, or that election timing drives policy. It puts three documented facts side by side: the condition, the government's own "Global Oil Shock" wording, and the 2029 term end.

## Excluded
- Any figure for the excise cut's revenue cost: estimates conflict, and there's no official number.
- The PIB release's "₹81.90/litre diesel under-recovery" figure: it's a March number, and it would confuse the October ₹34 estimate.
- All photographs of named politicians: rights are unverified and the brief's likeness concerns apply. See the production report.
