## 5. Workflow (how this was built, how to finish it)
1. `scripts/cutout.py` cuts both leads off the reference sheet. It upscales 2×, removes the white background (including white trapped between hair strands), and paints out only the eyes, brows and mouth.
   - The original eye and brow drawings become sprites that blink, raise, tilt and look around.
   - The mouth is redrawn in the same ink, so it lip-syncs from the audio level of each line.
2. `scripts/guide_vo.py` makes the guide reads.
3. `scripts/build_timeline.py` lays the lines out with comedy gaps. It writes `timeline.json`, `vo.wav` and per-frame mouth data.
4. `scripts/sfx.py` writes the sound stems, then `scripts/mix_stems.py` makes the −14 LUFS mix.
5. Remotion renders the reel; the composition is `Ep01` (`src/projects/ep01/`).

**To swap in the real voices:**
1. Top up ElevenLabs (about 2,000 credits), or record your own reads.
2. Save one file per line as `public/ep01/vo/<id>.wav` (b1…b6, g1…g8).
3. Run, from `avron-reels/`:
   ```
   python3 projects/2026-10-ep01-sasta-share/scripts/build_timeline.py projects/2026-10-ep01-sasta-share public/ep01/vo public/ep01
   python3 projects/2026-10-ep01-sasta-share/scripts/sfx.py projects/2026-10-ep01-sasta-share public/ep01
   python3 scripts/mix_stems.py public/ep01 projects/2026-10-ep01-sasta-share/gains.json
   node scripts/render.mjs Ep01 deliverables/2026-10-ep01-sasta-share/AVRON_ep01_FINAL_1080x1920.mp4
   ```

Everything re-times itself: lip-sync, cues, captions and camera.

**Gen-AI route (optional):** use the prompts in section 3 with an image-to-video model that takes a character reference (Kling or Veo). Drive the lip-sync from the final VO, then cut it to the timestamps in section 1. The code version above is consistent frame to frame, which these models often are not.

**Trims if the real VO still runs over 35 s** (your call; nothing cut yet): drop "Itna sasta," from b1, and shorten g5 to "Kitna kamaati hai, kitna karza hai, aur kitna pay kar raha hai. Yeh sab dekh!". That saves about 2.5 s.
