# AVRON script method (from the user, 2026-10-28)

The user's rule: **"This is how we will start building script."** Every AVRON script starts here, before hooks or any VO line. The user's three slides are saved next to this file (`1_audience_research.jpg`, `2_hook_value_solution_cta.jpg`, `3_cta_by_goal.jpg`). Their text, verbatim:

## Step 1: Identify

1. 10 common problems
2. 10 frustrations
3. 10 desired outcomes
4. 10 repeated questions
5. 10 common mistakes
6. 10 objections or limiting beliefs
7. Emotional reasons behind these problems
8. Likely phrases/language the audience uses

## Step 2: Structure

**HOOK**
→ Grab attention immediately.

**VALUE**
→ Explain the problem, mistake, misconception, or insight.
→ Make the viewer feel understood.
→ Give useful information, not filler.

**SOLUTION**
→ Give a clear, actionable solution.
→ Use steps/framework/examples when appropriate.

**CTA**
→ Give ONE clear next action.
→ Match the selected reel goal.
→ If the goal is followers, give a compelling reason to follow.
→ If comments, ask an easy but meaningful question.
→ If saves, make it worth revisiting.
→ If shares, identify who should receive it.
→ If leads/sales, create a natural next step without sounding desperate.

## How AVRON applies it

- **Audience:** Indian retail investors who watch finance reels in Hinglish (AVRON page). The Step 1 lists are written for **this reel's topic**, from real sources (Agent Reach read-only: Reddit r/IndiaInvestments and r/IndianStockMarket, YouTube and Instagram comments on the topic, Zerodha Varsity/TradingQnA threads, Google "people also ask"). Item 8 records the audience's words as they write them (Hinglish, slang, typos kept), and the VO borrows from it. Never invent quotes. If a list has fewer than 10 real items, say how many were found rather than padding.
- **Reel goal comes first.** Before Step 2, fix ONE goal: followers, comments, saves, shares, or leads/sales. Ask the user; in autonomous mode the default is **saves** for explainers and **comments** for debates. The CTA and the end card follow the goal.
- **Solution is education, not advice.** In AVRON, an "actionable solution" is a check, a framework or a question the viewer can apply themselves ("filing mein yeh line dekho", "3-step screen"). Never a buy/sell/hold call, a price target or a return promise.
- **Step 2 maps onto the brand v2 reel:** Hook = crop-in pull-out on the hook number; Value = the problem or misconception, with the receipt as proof; Solution = the steps as Rows or a panel morph; CTA = the end card.
- **End card by goal** (BRAND.md move 7): comments → Poll card with an easy question; saves → a "Checklist" card the viewer will want to come back to, then `Saved to AVRON Watchlist`; shares → "Bhejo us dost ko jo …" naming who should get it; followers → the reason to follow (what the next reel in the series answers); leads → one natural next step, no begging.
