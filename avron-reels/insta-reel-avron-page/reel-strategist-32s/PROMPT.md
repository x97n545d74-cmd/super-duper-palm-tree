CONSTRAINTS:

- Language: Hinglish voiceover - 70% Hindi, 30% English. Easy words.
- Hook: First 6 words on-screen must be a Hinglish question that stops scroll. Example: "Palantir war se billion kaise banata?"
- Structure:
0-3s HOOK: Question + pattern interrupt
3-7s PROOF: Real number from SEC/SEBI filing
7-14s SIMPLE EXPLANATION: Explain business like to a 10-year-old using 3 icons
14-21s MONEY SHOT: How much per person/product
21-28s SHOCK: Profit margin vs normal company

- Add 1 retention trick: At 18s, flash text "WAIT FOR PROFIT MARGIN" or "95% log ye miss karte hain"
