# Chandi kam pad rahi hai, phir girti kyun gayi? The silver crash, in Hinglish
AVRON · YouTube · 1920×1080 · ~12.5 min · Hinglish VO, Roman script on screen, no full stops in on-screen text

Reference video: "Silver Is Running Out... So Why Is It Crashing?" by Why X (youtu.be/ayTQn2jGtS0). We keep its question and its flow-vs-pile idea. Characters, jokes, analogies and title are our own: Gupta ji, Rohan, tamatar, FD and payal replace theirs.

Fact IDs in [brackets] point to the fact list at the end. Visual direction is in *italics*. Characters are fictional and labelled "Illustration" on screen. No buy, sell or hold call anywhere.

---

## Phase 1: Why the reference video worked, and how we use it

- **The hook is a contradiction.** Five years of shortage and a 55% fall sit in the same sentence. The viewer stays to see how both are true. We open on the same two facts, with Indian numbers next to them.
- **Two people carry the money lesson.** Their coin collector and their futures trader make leverage feel personal. Ours are Gupta ji (63, Indore, a silver coin every Dhanteras) and his nephew Rohan (27, Pune, MCX silver on his phone).
- **Every term gets a home analogy.** Margin is a security deposit, the deficit is a salary-vs-FD gap, thrifting is less tamatar in the sabzi.
- **Data and people alternate.** A Silver Institute number, then a family scene, then a Deutsche Bank number. No section runs on data alone for more than about 40 seconds.
- **What we add.** The Indian silver ETF premium trap of January 2026, which the reference video doesn't have, and a Dhanteras close, since Dhanteras falls on 6 Nov this year [D1].

Arc: Hook → teen shak → Gupta ji vs Rohan → 30 January → India ka apna trap → flow vs pile → solar ka twist → close

## Phase 3: Script

### 00:00 Hook (about 50 s)

*Black screen. One silver coin drops onto a brass thali, a clean metallic ring. Hold. A red price line draws itself across the frame and falls.*

**VO:** Paanch saal se duniya jitni chandi use kar rahi hai, khaanon se utni nikal hi nahi rahi. Har saal kami. Aur January ke peak se aaj tak, chandi ka daam aadhe se bhi zyada gir chuka hai. [S1] [P1]

**ON SCREEN:** 5 saal ki kami · 55% ki giraavat

**VO:** Yeh dono baatein ek saath sach nahi honi chahiye. School mein ek hi rule yaad rehta hai: cheez kam, daam zyada. 2023 mein tamatar ₹100 kilo gaya tha, wahi rule tha. [S1]

*Archive-style photo of a sabzi mandi tomato pile (stock, licensed), price board flipping up.*

**VO:** Aur yeh kami kisi WhatsApp forward ki kahani nahi hai. Yeh Silver Institute ka data hai, jo duniya ki har khaan aur har factory ki chandi ginta hai. 2026 lagaataar chhatha saal hoga jab demand supply se zyada rahegi. [S1]

**VO:** To jo cheez kam pad rahi hai, woh itni buri tarah giri kaise? Aaj yahi samjhenge, aur samjhenge ki is crash mein paisa kisne gawaaya aur kisne nahi.

### 00:50 Teen shak (about 1 min 30 s)

*Three cards slide in on a dark wall. Each one gets a thin red strike-through when the VO rules it out.*

**ON SCREEN:** 01 · Kami nakli hai

**VO:** Mera pehla shak tha ki shayad kami hi nakli hai. Par Silver Institute ke numbers Metals Focus banata hai, khaan aur factory ek-ek karke. 2025 mein kami 4 crore oz se zyada thi, 2026 ke liye anuman 4.6 crore oz ka hai. Kami asli hai. [S1]

**ON SCREEN:** 02 · Sab Fed ka khel hai

**VO:** Doosra shak, America ka Fed. Thoda sach hai. 30 January ko khabar aayi ki Kevin Warsh agle Fed chairman honge. Market ne socha rates jaldi nahi girenge, dollar upar gaya. Aur jo cheez byaaj nahi deti, jaise sona aur chandi, woh dabav mein aati hai. [F1]

**VO:** Par usi din sona lagbhag 11% gira aur chandi 31%. Khabar ek, din ek, aur chandi ko teen guna maar padi. Fed ne shuruaat ki, par itni badi giraavat akele Fed ki wajah se nahi thi. [F2]

**ON SCREEN:** Sona −11% · Chandi −31%

**ON SCREEN:** 03 · Ab koi chandi nahi chahta

**VO:** Teesra shak, ki logon ka mann bhar gaya. Ulta hua. 2026 mein coins aur bars ki demand 18% badhne ka anuman hai. [S2]

**VO:** Matlab kami bhi asli, crash bhi asli. Dono saath kaise chal rahe hain, yeh samajhne ke liye ek parivaar ki Diwali dekhte hain.

### 02:20 Gupta ji aur Rohan (about 2 min 20 s)

*Paper-craft illustration, labelled "Illustration". Left: an Indore drawing room, a steel almirah. Right: a Pune flat, a phone glowing on a bed.*

**ON SCREEN:** Illustration · kirdaar kaalpanik hain

**VO:** Yeh hain Gupta ji. 63 saal, Indore, railway se retire. Har Dhanteras ko sarafa bazaar jaate hain, das gram ka ek chandi ka sikka lete hain, aur almirah ke locker mein rakh dete hain. Gupta ji ne zindagi mein kabhi chandi ka daam app pe check nahi kiya.

*The almirah locker opens. A small stack of coins, each with a different year stamped on it.*

**VO:** Aur yeh hain unke bhatije Rohan. 27 saal, Pune mein job, YouTube pe trading videos 2x speed pe dekhta hai. Uska ek Telegram group hai, "Chandi Squad". Rohan ke paas bhi chandi hai, par usne use kabhi chhua nahi. Uske paas MCX pe silver futures ka contract hai.

*Rohan's phone: a clean, unbranded trading screen. A green number ticks up.*

**VO:** Futures ka matlab hai ek vaada, ki aage ek tay daam pe chandi khareedenge ya bechenge. Aur isme pura paisa nahi lagta. Aap ek chhota hissa jama karte ho, jaise kiraye ke ghar ka security deposit. Isko margin kehte hain. Baaki paisa udhaar ka hota hai.

**VO:** Ab ek seedha hisaab. Gupta ji ne ₹1 lakh ke sikke liye. Chandi 30% giri, to sikke ₹70,000 ke reh gaye. Gupta ji so gaye.

**ON SCREEN:** Gupta ji · ₹1 lakh → ₹70,000

**VO:** Rohan ne ₹1 lakh margin daala aur ₹10 lakh ki chandi control ki. Chandi 30% giri, to nuksaan ₹3 lakh. Uska ₹1 lakh poora gaya, aur upar se ₹2 lakh ka ghaata, agar broker ne beech mein position nahi kaati. [M1]

**ON SCREEN:** Rohan · ₹1 lakh margin · ₹3 lakh ka nuksaan

*Rohan's phone buzzes. A notification slides down: "Margin shortfall. Add funds."*

**VO:** Is message ka naam hai margin call. Matlab deposit kam pad gaya hai, abhi paisa daalo, warna hum tumhari position jo bhi daam screen pe hai uspe bech denge. Rohan se poocha nahi jaata.

### 04:40 30 January (about 2 min)

*Music drops to a low pulse. A chart line climbs steeply through 2025, labelled with real dates.*

**VO:** Pichhle saal ke doosre hisse mein Rohan genius lag raha tha. Chandi har hafte naya high bana rahi thi. 27 January ko MCX pe chandi ₹3.59 lakh kilo ke record pe thi, aur 29 January ko international daam $121 ke upar. Chandi Squad mein roz screenshot aate the. [P2] [P1]

**ON SCREEN:** ₹3.59 lakh/kg · 27 Jan · record

**VO:** Usse pehle December mein CME, jahan duniya ke sabse bade silver futures chalte hain, ek hafte mein do baar margin badha chuka tha. Matlab har Rohan ko ek hi position ke liye zyada deposit chahiye tha. [F3]

**VO:** Phir aaya Friday, 30 January. Warsh ki khabar, dollar upar, aur silver futures ek din mein 31% gire. 1980 ke baad chandi ka sabse bura din. [F2]

*The chart line breaks and falls. Each step down lights up a small paper figure that topples into the next.*

**VO:** Ab jo log bechna nahi chahte the, unhe bechna pada. Deposit khatam hua, system ne position kaat di, aur har zabardasti ki sale ne daam aur giraaya. Isse agle aadmi ka deposit khatam hua, aur uski bhi sale hui. Ek ke baad ek, aur har ek ke phone pe wahi message tha jo Rohan ke phone pe tha.

*Cut to an archival-style newspaper treatment (redrawn by us, no agency photos): "Silver Thursday · 27 March 1980".*

**VO:** Yeh pehli baar nahi hua. 1980 mein America ke Hunt bhaiyon ne udhaar ke paise se itni chandi khareedi ki daam aasmaan chhoo gaye. Phir exchange ne rules badle, margin badhe, aur 27 March 1980 ko daam aisa gira ki us din ko aaj bhi Silver Thursday kehte hain. [H1]

**VO:** Aaj Hunt bhaiyon ki jagah Rohan jaise chhote traders hain, aur dono baar giraavat udhaar ke paise ne tez ki.

### 06:40 India ka apna trap: ETF premium (about 1 min 10 s)

*A clean bar chart: two bars per day, "NAV" and "Bazaar daam". The second bar grows past the first.*

**VO:** Aur India mein ek aur trap tha, jo Rohan jaise traders tak simit nahi tha. Silver ETF. Yeh mutual fund jaisa hai, jiske peeche asli chandi rakhi hoti hai. Iska ek asli mol hota hai, NAV.

**VO:** January mein itne log ek saath silver ETF pe toot pade ki January mein silver ETFs mein ₹9,463 crore aaye. Kai ETFs apne NAV se 9 se 12% upar bik rahe the. Matlab ₹100 ki chandi ke liye log ₹110 se zyada de rahe the. [E1] [E2]

**ON SCREEN:** NAV ₹100 · Bazaar daam ₹112

**VO:** Phir 30 January ko ulta hua. Kuch silver ETFs apne NAV se 11.6% neeche bik gaye. Jisne premium pe khareeda aur discount pe becha, use chandi ki giraavat ke upar se yeh maar alag se padi. [E3]

**VO:** Yahan kisi ne udhaar nahi liya tha. Bheed ne daam NAV se upar kheencha, aur bheed hat-te hi neeche.

### 07:50 Kami aur bhandaar (about 2 min)

*Two clean graphics side by side. Left, a thin stream labelled "Har saal ki kami". Right, a huge still reservoir labelled "Jama chandi".*

**VO:** Rohan ka account saaf ho gaya aur Chandi Squad chup ho gaya. Par ek sawaal bachta hai. Agar crash sirf udhaar ki wajah se tha, to forced selling khatam hone ke baad daam wapas kyun nahi uchhla? Kami to ab bhi thi.

**VO:** Kami aapko sirf har saal ka hisaab batati hai. Kitni chandi pehle se jama padi hai, yeh nahi batati.

**VO:** Apne ghar se samjhiye. Salary ₹40,000, kharcha ₹41,000. Har mahine ₹1,000 ki kami. Par bank mein ₹3 lakh ki FD padi hai. Aap gareeb nahi ho rahe, bas dheere-dheere kam ameer ho rahe ho. Yeh kami saalon tak chal sakti hai.

**ON SCREEN:** Kami ₹1,000/mahina · FD ₹3 lakh

**VO:** Chandi ke saath bhi yahi hai. Duniya har saal thodi zyada use karti hai. Par insaan hazaaron saal se chandi jama kar raha hai, bank vaults mein, exchange ke godaamon mein, factoriyon ke stock mein, aur haan, hamari daadi-naani ki almirah mein bhi.

*Slow pan across a dark vault photo (licensed stock), cold light.*

**VO:** Deutsche Bank ke hisaab se August 2026 ke end mein sirf London ke vaults mein 91 crore oz se zyada chandi thi. Isme se 30 crore oz se zyada aisi thi jo khulle mein khareedi ja sakti hai. Yeh 2026 ki poori kami ka lagbhag saadhe chhe guna hai. [V1]

**ON SCREEN:** London · 91 crore oz · free 30 crore oz+ · kami ka ~6.5 guna

**VO:** Aur jab daam aasmaan pe gaye, to chhupi hui chandi bhi bahar aayi. Purani payal, bichhiya, puja ki thaali, purane bartan, factory ka scrap. Sab pighalne gaya. 2025 mein recycling 13 saal ke high pe pahunchi. [S3]

*A pair of old silver anklets on a jeweller's scale, then a crucible glow (stock).*

### 09:50 Solar ka twist (about 1 min 40 s)

*Macro shots of a solar cell, the thin silver lines on its face (licensed stock). Then a paper-craft factory line.*

**VO:** Ab chandi ke sabse bade kharidaar ki baat.

**VO:** Hum chandi ko gehne aur sikkon se jodte hain. Par duniya ki aadhi se zyada chandi industry use karti hai: phone, gaadi, aur sabse zyada solar panel. Har solar cell pe chandi ki patli lakeerein hoti hain jo bijli ikattha karti hain. [S4]

**VO:** Ab 2023 yaad kijiye, jab tamatar ₹100 kilo gaya. Ghar mein khaana band nahi hua. Sabzi mein tamatar thoda kam pada, kahin imli aa gayi, kahin dahi. Aur kai gharon mein daam girne ke baad bhi tamatar utna wapas nahi aaya.

**VO:** Solar ke saath yahi hua. Early 2026 mein solar module banane ke kharche ka 30% se zyada sirf chandi ka tha. Ek saal pehle yeh 10% se kam tha. [T1]

**ON SCREEN:** Module ki laagat mein chandi · 10% se kam → 30% se zyada

**VO:** To engineers ne lakeerein aur patli kar di, paste mein taamba milaya, aur design badla taaki kam chandi lage. Ise thrifting kehte hain. Deutsche Bank ka anuman hai ki 2026 mein har solar cell mein chandi 17% kam lagegi, aur solar ki kul chandi demand 20% se zyada giregi. [T2]

**ON SCREEN:** Har cell mein chandi −17%

**VO:** Aur factory jo tareeka ek baar seekh leti hai, woh daam girne pe bhool nahi jaati. Isliye Deutsche Bank 2027 mein chandi ko kami se nikal kar surplus mein jaata dekh raha hai. Yeh ek bank ka anuman hai, pakki baat nahi. [T2]

**VO:** To chandi ke is crash mein koi saazish nahi thi. Kami saalon ke hisaab se chalti hai, khaan ki speed se. Daam minuton mein chalta hai, margin call ki speed se. Nuksaan un logon ka hua jinhone udhaar lekar saalon wali kahani pe hafton ka daav lagaya.

**VO:** Holi tak Rohan ne trading app delete kar diya tha aur Chandi Squad chhod diya tha. Gupta ji ne us Jan-Feb mein ek baar bhi locker nahi khola.

*The almirah locker, a new coin dated 2026 added to the stack.*

**VO:** Is saal Dhanteras 6 November ko hai. Bahut log chandi lenge, kuch pooja ke liye, kuch nivesh samajh kar. Hum kisi ko kharidne ya na kharidne ki salaah nahi dete. Bas jab koi kahe ki "yeh cheez khatam ho rahi hai, abhi le lo", to do sawaal poochiye. [D1]

**ON SCREEN:** 1 · Jama bhandaar kitna bada hai
**ON SCREEN:** 2 · Kharidne wale kiska paisa laga rahe hain

**VO:** Pehla, jo pehle se jama hai woh kitna bada hai. Doosra, kharidne wale apna paisa laga rahe hain ya udhaar ka. Agar bhandaar bada hai aur kharidaar udhaar pe hain, to sirf kami ke bharose daam upar nahi tikta.

**VO:** Agli baar kis cheez ki kahani todein, comment mein likhiye. Main hoon AVRON, milte hain agle video mein.

**ON SCREEN:** Yeh salaah nahi hai · sirf jaankaari ke liye

## Editing notes

- **Sound.** A low riser under Rohan's margin call. A dry metallic ring each time Gupta ji's coin appears. No whooshes or impacts beyond the 30 January drop.
- **Numbers on screen.** Big numbers (31%, ₹3.59 lakh, 91 crore oz, 17%) appear as masked type reveals for about 2 seconds, Inter, tight tracking, one accent colour. No shake, glow or particles (house motion rules).
- **Illustration.** Gupta ji and Rohan are paper-craft figures in the Swiggy film style (`src/projects/swiggy/film/Paper.tsx`), labelled "Illustration". No AI likeness of real people. The Hunt brothers appear only as a redrawn newspaper headline, no photos.
- **Images.** Only licensed or free-licence stock, logged in `projects/2026-10-silver-crash/assets/LICENSES.md`.
- **VO.** Hinglish read with a correct Indian accent. The user's own ElevenLabs voice (Tia Mirza, v4) is the first choice; check credits before generating. About 1,600 words of VO, roughly 11.5–12.5 min at a natural Hinglish pace with the visual pauses.
