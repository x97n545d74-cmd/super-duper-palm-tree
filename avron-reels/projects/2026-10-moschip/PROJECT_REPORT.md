## 1. Sector: global semiconductors
- WSTS (Spring 2026 forecast, 2 Jun 2026): the market grows **90% in 2026 to $1.51 trillion** and **27% in 2027 to ~$1.9 trillion** [P3].
- **The boom is concentrated:** Memory is forecast at **~+250%** (over $800 bn). Logic +37%, microprocessors +20%, analog +10% [P3]. AI infrastructure and HBM drive it.
- **AVRON interpretation:** a headline "chip boom" doesn't lift every chip company equally. Memory makers capture most of 2026's growth, while design-services firms are paid per project and per milestone.

## 2. Industry: India's chip-design ecosystem
- India has **~20% of the global semiconductor design workforce**. PIB restates this, attributing it to "industry estimates" [P4].
- **DLI scheme** (part of the 2021 ₹76,000 cr semiconductor programme): up to **50% of eligible design expenditure, capped at ₹15 cr per application**, plus deployment incentives of **6–4% of net sales** (capped at ₹30 cr) [P5/S5].
- **Semicon 2.0** (Cabinet, Jul 2026, ₹1,27,500 cr outlay) lists **chip design as the first pillar** [S6, secondary].
- **AVRON interpretation:** India has always had the designers. The policy push is to turn design *services* into Indian-owned *products and IP*. MosChip is trying to make exactly that transition.

## 3. Company
### 3.1 Business model
| Unit | What it sells | How revenue lands |
|---|---|---|
| **Silicon Engineering: Turnkey ASIC and design services** | Designing and delivering a customer's entire chip (spec → RTL → verification → physical design → tape-out → production support) | **Milestone-based**: lumpy by design. The company states that "fluctuations in revenue are inherent to the Turnkey ASIC business" [P2] |
| **Product Engineering Services (PES)** | Embedded systems, software, AI/ML, system design (much of it from the Softnautics acquisition, merged April 2025) | Order-based; longer sales cycles currently [P2] |

**Recent credibility markers** (company filings) [P1]:
- About **100 tape-outs in FY26** (including test chips, respins and derivatives).
- **Custom SoC delivered to ISRO's Space Applications Centre** for the satellite navigation programme.
- A **5nm HPC processor for C-DAC**, built with Socionext, "taping out soon".
- An edge-AI SoC implementation with EMASS (Singapore).
- PES wins from **two Fortune 500** companies.
- **12 new customers in FY26** (Australia, Europe, Japan, South Korea).

**Own-product bet: VIDYUT (VIDYUT100)**
- A smart energy-meter IC (polyphase metrology, RISC-V based) developed under DLI. Approved June 2024 [S4].
- **Taped out, and initial bring-up results described as "promising"** [P1]. Validation is still ongoing.
- No revenue from it has been disclosed yet.

**Segments** [S3, secondary reading of the filing]:

- **PES:** ₹18.41 cr (vs ₹32.86 cr YoY), with a **segment loss of ~₹2.6 cr**.

**Management's explanation** [P2]: turnkey engagements moved to the tape-out stage, so less revenue was recognised. PES conversion has been slower than anticipated. "Delivery capabilities… remain unchanged."

### 3.4 Balance sheet and cash [S1]
- Borrowings: ₹69 cr (FY24) → ₹21 cr (FY25) → **₹88 cr (FY26)**.
- Operating cash flow: ₹31 → ₹100 → **₹93 cr**. Cash generation is positive.
- **Debtor days: 134 (FY26).** More than four months of revenue is waiting to be collected, which is typical for milestone billing but worth watching.

### 3.5 Valuation and ownership (facts, not a view)
- ~₹3,216 cr market cap on FY26 revenue of ₹585 cr gives **~5.5× sales**. Trailing P/E is ~120×. Both are AVRON calculations.
- **Institutional ownership is close to nil** (FII 0.36%, DII 0%). The stock is largely retail-held, which can amplify volatility.

## 4. What to watch (catalysts and tests)
1. **Q2 FY27 results:** last year's Q2 board meeting was on 24 Oct 2025. The Q2 FY27 date is **not yet confirmed**. Does turnkey revenue rebound after the tape-outs?
2. **PES:** can it return to profit? It posted a segment loss in Q1.
3. **VIDYUT:** completion of validation, then the first commercial orders. Smart-meter demand ties into India's metering rollout.
4. **The C-DAC 5nm HPC tape-out**, a flagship credibility event.
5. **Receivables and borrowings:** do debtor days fall from 134?

## 5. Risks
- **Lumpiness:** milestone revenue makes individual quarters unreliable signals.
- **Concentration risk:** customer concentration is not disclosed in what I reviewed (open question).
- **Margin compression** during the scale-up (OPM 15% → 10%).
- **Product risk:** a first own chip faces validation, certification and commercial-adoption hurdles.
- **Valuation and liquidity:** a high multiple on falling quarterly profit, with negligible institutional ownership.
- **No formal guidance**, so expectations aren't anchored.

## 6. Open questions / not verified
- Q2 FY27 board-meeting date.
- Customer concentration and the geographic revenue split.
- Expected VIDYUT revenue timing or volume, and the DLI incentive amount approved.
- Segment figures for Q1 come from a secondary reading of the filing. They need confirming against the exchange segment note before publishing.
