## 1. Sector: the power grid build-out
- **Transmission:** the National Electricity Plan (Transmission) costs **₹9.15 lakh crore for 2023–2032** (220 kV and above). The network grows to ~6.48 lakh ckm and transformation capacity to ~2,345 GVA, for a projected 458 GW peak demand [P3].
- **Distribution:** RDSS has a **₹3,03,758 crore** outlay (FY22–FY26). ₹2.8 lakh crore of projects are approved, and 4.05 crore smart meters are installed under RDSS (as of 15 Jan 2026) [P4].
- **Storage:** BESS VGF schemes total about 43 GWh. On **30 Sep 2026 the Cabinet approved Green Energy Corridor Phase-III, with ₹50,000 cr for 50 GWh of BESS** within a ₹1,86,405 cr outlay [P5].
- **AVRON interpretation:** demand for grid EPC is policy-backed and multi-year. For a contractor, though, the business is won in tenders and survived on working capital.

## 2. Industry: state utility EPC (how the money moves)
- The customers are state discoms and transcos (e.g. MGVCL, DGVCL, PGVCL in Gujarat), won through L1 (lowest-bid) tenders [S2].
- Execution is front-loaded on materials and labour, while payment follows milestones and inspection. That makes **receivables and working-capital funding the core constraint**, not demand.
- Bigger tenders (TBCB, ₹1,000–2,000 cr) need larger balance sheets. Management says these are "lesser margin" for the next four to five years [P1].

## 3. Company
### 3.1 What Viviana does [P1]
- **EPC up to 400 kV:** substations, lines and distribution-network upgrades.
- **Order book: ₹1,312.53 cr** (Q1 FY27 call). That's more than ₹800 cr in EPC alone, and more than ₹1,300 cr including BESS (implying roughly ₹500 cr of BESS; AVRON derivation).
- **Pipeline:** "unexecuted order booking plus L1" is about **₹840 cr**. More than **₹1,400 cr** of bids are under evaluation, and more than ₹2,600 cr are being bid.
- **Transformers:** a greenfield plant with **phase-1 capex of about ₹90 cr**, of which **₹3.5–4 cr has been spent so far**. It starts at 132 kV / 63 MVA, then 220 and 400 kV. An "IDT transformer facility" was to be inaugurated by end-August 2026, which I haven't verified.
- **First order in 2014 was ₹21 lakh.** The single largest bid capacity is now over ₹120 cr.

### 3.3 The pattern under the growth
**Revenue lands at year-end** (quarterly revenue, ₹ cr) [S1]:
| Jun-25 | Sep-25 | Dec-25 | **Mar-26** | Jun-26 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 31.68 | 58.94 | 117.87 | **322.75** | 72.39 |
- **61% of FY26 revenue came in Q4** (322.75 / 531; AVRON calc.). Management confirmed that "the major revenue came in the month of March" [P1].

**Cash hasn't followed profit** [S1]:
| | FY24 | FY25 | FY26 |
|---|---|---|---|
| Cash from operations | −3 | −14 | **−4** |
| Borrowings | 17 | 51 | **100** |
| Debtor days | 153 | 207 | **267** |
- **Three straight years of negative operating cash flow** while net profit rose from ₹7 cr to ₹53 cr.
- **Management's explanation** [P1]: there are no delayed payments from any client, and more than ₹40 cr of the 30-June receivables were collected in July. Secured NCDs were raised as "a contingency source of liquidity" because enhancement of the bank working-capital limits was "taking longer time than expected" (Dec–Jan).

### 3.4 Q1 FY27 (Apr–Jun 2026)
- Company figures [P1/P2]: revenue **₹71.86 cr (+246% YoY)**, EBITDA ₹11.56 cr (16%+ margin), PAT **₹6.93 cr**.
- Screener's consolidated figures: ₹72.39 cr revenue and ₹6.02 cr profit [S1]. **The difference needs reconciling against the filing** before publishing.

### 3.5 Corporate structure
- Acquired **75% of Aarsh Transformers** (17 Sep 2024) and **90% of Viviana Life Spaces**, a real-estate company (28 Mar 2025) [S4].
- In **Aug 2026 the board approved divesting both**, to focus "exclusively on its core power infrastructure business". Management says this is not an exit from transformers [P1].
- **Open question:** buyer, consideration and related-party status aren't confirmed in what I reviewed.

## 4. What to watch
1. **Q2 FY27 (Jul–Sep) revenue:** does execution spread through the year, or does it pile up in March again? The run-rate needed for ₹875–910 cr is about ₹270 cr a quarter for the rest of the year (AVRON calc.: (875 − 72) / 3 ≈ ₹268 cr).
2. **Operating cash flow turning positive,** and debtor days falling from 267.
3. **Borrowings and NCDs,** and whether the bank working-capital enhancement was approved.
4. **Transformer plant progress:** ₹90 cr capex, ~₹4 cr spent so far.
5. **The divestment terms** for Viviana Life Spaces and Aarsh.
6. **Any equity raise:** management said developments "including the investment from the promoters" would be published.

## 5. Risks
- **Working-capital intensity:** negative operating cash flow, 267 debtor days and rising debt.
- **Q4 concentration:** a single quarter's slippage can miss the annual guidance.
- **Client concentration** in state utilities (largely Gujarat discoms in the order flow I reviewed).
- **Margin pressure** in bigger tenders (management's own caveat).
- **Execution of a new manufacturing vertical** (transformers, BESS).
- **Liquidity:** a recently migrated small cap with ~1% institutional ownership.
