       Pursuant to Regulation 30 of the SEBI (Listing Obligations and Disclosure
       Requirements) Regulations, 2015 (“SEBI LODR Regulations”), we are pleased to inform
       you that Viviana Power Tech Limited (“the Company”) has today, i.e. 21st September,
       2026, formally inaugurated its Power Transformer Manufacturing Facility at Por,
       Vadodara, Gujarat.

       The facility represents an important step in the Company’s strategic initiative to expand
       its presence from power transmission and distribution EPC activities into power
       equipment manufacturing, thereby strengthening its capabilities across the power
       infrastructure value chain.

       The facility has been established with a focus on manufacturing power transformers up
       to 20 MVA rating in the 66 kV and 33 kV classes, with an annual production capacity up
       to 1,000 MVA, and also provide facility to manufacture allied power equipment to cater
       to the growing requirements of the renewable energy, transmission and other power
       infrastructure sectors.

       The Company expects the facility to provide the following strategic benefits:
       - Backward and forward integration of the Company’s Power T&D EPC business;
       - Enhancement of in-house manufacturing capabilities for power transformers;
       - Ability to cater to the increasing demand for power equipment arising from power
          transmission, distribution and renewable energy segment;
       - Potential improvement in project execution capabilities and supply-chain
          efficiencies;
       - Broadening Company’s business portfolio in power equipment manufacturing
          segment and creation of an additional revenue stream; and;
       - Strengthening of the Company’s position as an integrated Power T&D infrastructure
          and equipment solutions provider.
