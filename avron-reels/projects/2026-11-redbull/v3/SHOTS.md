# The Can: v3 visual concept (2026-11-06)

The user rejected the v2 cards and charts ("the same basic thing everybody uses"). New rule for this reel: **everything comes out of the can.** One real-time 3D can (three.js) is the hero of all ten scenes. File: `film.html` (seek(t), 3:00); render with `node render.mjs mp4 <out> <from> <to> 30`.

| Time | Set piece | What the can does |
|---|---|---|
| 0:00–0:10 | The pop | Drops in and lands. On "doesn't" the ring-pull pops and a jet of CO2 gas bursts out. The headline forms in the gas and BEVERAGE gets a crimson strike. The camera rises over the opening and dives in |
| 0:10–0:25 | Screens up and down | Left: 30-second ad screens rise from the dark, count down and sink, over and over, while coins drain ($5.1B, Coca-Cola 2024). Right: owned screens (your photos) telescope up out of the open can and stay up (€11.2B) |
| 0:25–0:35 | X-ray | The can's skin turns transparent with a yellow wireframe. Inside are floors like a building: teams and events, camera crews, edit bay, live broadcast. Your production-room photo sits on one floor. The drink is only the thin glowing layer at the bottom |
| 0:35–0:50 | The fill | Your neon chart shrinks into the can. A coin drops in, and the cost layer fills a 4% sliver ($0.15 of $3.50, est.). The rest fills dark red. ≈23× |
| 0:50–1:15 | The wheel | The can tips over and becomes the axle of a yellow flywheel with 4 nodes. The spin ramps up hard, and every lap fires cans out of the rim |
| 1:15–1:35 | Can to space | A balloon lifts the can to 128,100 ft with the altitude counting and the curve of the Earth below (≈291,000 cans stacked). Then a globe lights up with viewers as the counter hits 8,000,000+ |
| 1:35–1:55 | The scale | Big 3D numbers $0 / $30–50M / $100s of M. Then a balance scale: gold bars (cost, est.) against a can with a pile of screens (exposure, est.). It tips toward exposure |
| 1:55–2:15 | The fall | The can free-falls through clouds with a speed ramp: fast, then slow-mo, then fast. "No product pitch." Your cliff photo flies past. "The capsule / the suit / the stream" |
| 2:15–2:40 | Three cans | Three cans pop one after another as the camera trucks along them. Each one raises a screen with one step of the playbook |
| 2:40–3:00 | Crush to follow | "Don't build ads. Build a media brand." The can is crushed flat and flips into a 3D Follow button. A cursor taps it, sparks burst, and it hard-cuts at 3:00 |

Glue: a post shader on every frame does barrel distortion, edge chromatic aberration, halation, anamorphic streaks, 35 mm grain, dust, faint scanlines and a vignette. There are 9 impact frames, inside WCAG's 3-flashes-a-second limit.

The can is a Red Bull-style design (blue/silver checker, yellow disc, wordmark in type). It is not Red Bull's logo or real can art, and there are no bulls.
