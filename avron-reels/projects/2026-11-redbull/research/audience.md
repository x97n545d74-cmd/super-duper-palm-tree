# Audience research (script method #9, step 1), 2026-11-06

**Audience:** English-speaking viewers interested in business and marketing, and founders who follow "how brands make money" content.
**Reel goal:** followers (the script's CTA is "hit follow").

## What was found
Searches for real Reddit threads on "Red Bull is a media company" and "how does Red Bull afford two F1 teams" turned up no threads I could read (the Reddit pages don't come through the search tool from this cloud IP). What did come up were articles and newsletters on the same questions, so the lists below are **short on purpose**. Fewer than 10 real items were found per list, and nothing is padded.

### Repeated questions (4 found)
1. How can Red Bull afford two Formula 1 teams? (Jalopnik headline: "Do You Have Any Idea How Red Bull Can Afford Two Formula One Teams?")
2. Why does Red Bull own a football club? (Athletic Interest, "Why Red Bull owns a football team"; a Coconote transcript of the same video)
3. Is Red Bull a media company that happens to sell drinks? (Jezebel, "How Red Bull Invented the SponCon Industrial Complex": artists telling a critic Red Bull is "actually a media company")
4. How much does Red Bull spend on marketing? (asked in many newsletters; Red Bull doesn't publish it)

### Misconceptions to correct (3 found)
1. "Red Bull doesn't advertise." It still runs TV spots (2025 Super Bowl).
2. "The drink is a side business." Energy drinks are still nearly all of the revenue (12.67 bn cans in 2024).
3. "Stratos was free media." There was no rights fee, but the project cost an estimated $30–50 m.

### Objection the reel must survive (1 found)
- Earned media value is a soft number; one commentator argues the real return can only be judged against the project's cost. So the scorecard shows cost and value side by side, both marked as estimates.

## Gap
Steps 1.1–1.8 (problems, frustrations, desired outcomes, mistakes, emotional reasons, audience phrases) need comment sections: YouTube comments on the "Red Bull marketing" explainers, and r/marketing and r/Entrepreneur threads. Run Agent Reach on those when the Reddit and YouTube readers aren't rate-limited, then tune the hook and the CTA.
