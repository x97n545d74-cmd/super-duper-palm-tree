# Nohandslabs: 15 s brand film storyboard

**Format:** 1920×1080 at 60 fps. Music at 128 BPM: 1 beat = 0.46875 s, 1 bar = 1.875 s, so 8 bars = 15.000 s. Every cut sits on a downbeat.

**Brand (taken from nohandslabs.com):**
- Colours: background `#07080a`, accent lime `#c6ff3d`, dim `#9d9ea1`, foreground `#ececed`.
- Type: Space Grotesk (display), JetBrains Mono (system), Doto (dot-matrix numerals), Geist (body). All are OFL fonts from Google Fonts.
- Motifs: the dot-matrix hand that "lets go" into lime fragments, a status bar, `[ bracket ]` buttons, `//` comments, and the 95% AI / 5% human ratio.

**Copy rule:** every line on screen is quoted or condensed from the site. The runtime numbers are labelled `[illustrative]`, as they are on the site.

**Rejected ideas:**
- Camera shake, RGB split and lens flares: off-brand, because the site is precise and minimal.
- A voiceover: it competes with the type, and the site's copy already does the talking.
- Stock footage: the brand is a system, not people.
