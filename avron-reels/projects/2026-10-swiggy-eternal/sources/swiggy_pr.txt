      Bengaluru, India, October 22, 2026


      Swiggy (Swiggy Ltd, NSE: SWIGGY / BSE: 544285), India’s pioneering on-demand convenience platform, reported
      results today for the quarter and half year ended September 30, 2026.

      "Our Food delivery business delivered its highest ever proﬁts and margins while we continue to unlock new
      category growth through Toing, our standalone oﬀering for aﬀordable meals. In quick commerce, we delivered
      accelerated growth as guided and obtained necessary approvals to qualify as an Indian owned and controlled
      company - an important milestone for the business with the transition underway. As we shared at our Capital
      Markets Day, we believe that diﬀerentiation will decide winners with speed and selection becoming table stakes.
      We see assortment as a curation game rather than a volume game, and our aim is to enable consumers to buy
      better, not just oﬀer more," said Sriharsha Majety, MD & Group CEO, Swiggy.

      Key ﬁnancial and operational highlights for the quarter:

      Swiggy’s Food Delivery business Gross Order Value (GOV) grew 17.7% YoY (+5.9% QoQ), to INR 10,054 crore. Food
      delivery MTUs grew 15.2% YoY to reach 19.8 million. Adjusted EBITDA improved by INR 101 Cr YoY to INR 341 Cr and
      3.4% Adjusted EBITDA margin (+58 bps YoY). Toing, the standalone app for aﬀordable, budget-friendly meals
      services in 50+ cities. Two out of three new users on Toing were new to the food delivery category itself, and the
      high-volume demand is helping a substantial number of restaurant partners achieve unprecedented economies of
      scale. Out-of-Home Consumption grew 34.9% YoY to INR 1,508 Cr and delivered seventh successive quarter of
      proﬁtability with margins at 0.5% of GOV.

      Quick Commerce NOV grew 31.4% YoY and 13.4% QoQ to INR 6,594 Cr. Instamart added 69 darkstores in the
      quarter, equal to the cumulative additions of the last three quarters, bringing the total to 1,240 stores covering
      5.2 million sq ft (+13.3% YoY). Contribution for the quarter stood at -1.0% of GOV, up 157 bps YoY and down 82 bps
      QoQ. Adjusted EBITDA margin improved to -9.7% from -9.8% in Q1FY27, posting an overall loss of INR 895 Cr for
      the quarter.

      About Swiggy

      Swiggy is India’s pioneering on-demand convenience platform, catering to millions of consumers each month.
      Founded in 2014, its mission is to elevate the quality of life for the urban consumer by offering unparalleled
      convenience, enabled by over 7.6 lakh delivery partners. With an extensive footprint in food delivery, Swiggy Food
      collaborates with over 2.7 lakh restaurants across 750+ cities. Instamart, its quick commerce platform operating in
      133 cities, delivers groceries and other essentials across 20+ categories. Fueled by a commitment to innovation,
      Swiggy continually incubates and integrates new services like Dineout and Swiggy Scenes into its multi-service app
      as well as creating standalone offerings like Toing and Crew for opening up new market segments. Leveraging
      cutting-edge technology and Swiggy One, the country’s only membership program offering benefits across food,
      quick commerce and dining out, Swiggy aims to provide a superior experience to its users.
