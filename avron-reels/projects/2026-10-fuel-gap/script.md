## Fact vs interpretation
- Per-litre losses are **estimates** by Jefferies and ICRA, with different dates and methods. They're never stated as fact.
- "The gap lands on the oil companies" is AVRON's interpretation. It follows from pump prices holding steady while the estimated losses grew.
- No company is named as a buy or sell, and there are no price predictions.
