# Crash ka wait? Theme nahi, layer dekho
Project: projects/2026-10-datacenter-pharma/ · Date: 2026-10-27 · Length: 60.0 s (VO 58.7 s + 1.3 s end card) · Prompts used: Viral Reel Engine (#8) for the loop, Motion Graphic One (hook → data → split → implication → CTA structure, red/green data colour code), Motion Graphic Two (house motion rules, beat cuts) · Motion refs: 09 kinetic type, 03/04 blue grid, 02 comic burst, 01 paper collage, 10 phone notifications, 12 bead ring

## 1. Winning Creative Angle
People waiting for a crash got a 16% correction this year, and the question now is what to buy. The brief's answer was "two immune sectors". The checked data says something more useful. Demand in data centres and pharma did hold up (vacancy 2.8%, pharma index up double digits over a year), but the stocks were not immune: Anant Raj was 40% below its peak by March, and India's semaglutide patent expiry on 20 March turned into a ten-company price race. So the reel teaches one idea: look at the layer of a theme, not the theme. Most theme reels list "data centre stocks". Showing one data centre as land → servers → cooling, with one company fact per layer, is the part other creators skip, and it keeps AVRON on the right side of advice rules.

## 2. Winning Hook
- Spoken: "Saalon se crash ka wait kar rahe the na? Is saal Nifty peak se 16% gira. Ab kharidna kya hai?"
- First frame text: CRASH KA WAIT?
- Opening visual: frame 0 is black with a thin white horizontal line at 62% height. "CRASH" (Inter Black, 200 px, white) hangs off the line on a pendulum and swings in from the left; "KA WAIT?" follows on the next beat, with "WAIT?" in AVRON red #E5484D. By frame 12 the full line is set and the pendulum settles with no bounce. The ticking synth starts on frame 0.
- Why it won: 88/100 after rewrite. It keeps the user's psychological callout and the "WAITING FOR A CRASH?" idea, drops the slang, and puts a checked number on screen by 4 s. The runner-up (#8, "1 data centre, 3 layers", 80) became the spine of the middle section.

## 4. Full Motion-Graphics Direction
- **Palette:** background #0A0A0B; type #FFFFFF; secondary type #8A8F98; red #E5484D (falls, the hook's "WAIT?"); green #3DD68C (pharma index only). No gold, no third accent. The paper collage keeps its cream paper (#EFE6D2) but sits on the same black frame.
- **Type:** Inter, tracking −2%. Hero numbers 180–220 px, labels 54–64 px, source tags 28 px JetBrains Mono at 60% white, always bottom-left above the Reels caption zone. Company names 64 px caps. No full stops on screen. Hindi in Roman script.
- **Safe zones:** keep all type inside `src/brand/tokens.ts` safe area (top 220 px and bottom 380 px clear of key info).
- **Camera language:** one language: slow push (2% per second) plus match moves (line → chart baseline, red line → grass strip, building zoom-out → phone desk). Tilt up through the collage building is the one vertical move.
- **Charts:** Nifty line uses two verified points (26,373 on 5 Jan, 22,180 on 8 Oct). The span between them is drawn **dashed** because the path in between isn't plotted from data. Pharma vs Nifty is a directional two-line sketch with no y-axis values; label says "direction, 1 saal". Anant Raj line uses the ₹744.1 peak and the −40% March level only, dashed between.
- **Transitions:** push cuts with real motion blur (8 frames), hard cuts on downbeats, one comic burst at 19.8 s. No glitch visuals: the brief asked for them, but flashes and glitch are on AVRON's banned list, so glitch lives in the sound only.
- **Hierarchy per frame:** one number, one label, one source tag. Never two numbers at hero size together.
- **Banned and absent:** camera shake, particles, glows, grid floors, flashes, bouncy easing, neon, fake tickers. The brief's "green upward ticker graphics" were cut; the one green chip is a dated, sourced move.
- **Code to reuse:** `musk/Reel.tsx` (B8 kinetic type, BlueGrid, Desk, Notif), `anchor/ReelV2.tsx` (S2b burst, Collage), `footy/Reel.tsx` (Cta bead ring). New: rack-fill icon, pill icon, collage data-centre cross-section, price-tag strike.
