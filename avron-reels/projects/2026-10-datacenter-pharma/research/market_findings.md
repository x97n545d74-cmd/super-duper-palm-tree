## Verdict on the hook

1. "The opportunity came" holds up: the Nifty fell about 16% from its 5 Jan 2026 high (26,373) to its 8 Oct 2026 low (22,180), and it is still about 13% down YTD and about 14% below its peak. That is a correction, not a crash. Don't call it a "crash" on screen; March 2020 (-38%) was the crash.
2. "You got scared" does not hold up as a claim about retail in aggregate. SIP inflows hit a record ₹32,490 cr in Sep 2026, the stoppage ratio fell for the 5th month in a row, and DIIs bought ₹6.2 lakh cr in 2026. Foreigners are the ones selling (record $32.56 bn YTD). It only works as a direct question to one viewer ("kya aap darr gaye?"), not as a statement of fact.
3. "Five years" is off: the last crash was 6.5 years ago. Use "saalon se" or "2020 ke baad se".
4. A better verified anchor: "Nifty peak se ~16% gira, phir bhi Indians ne Sep mein record ₹32,490 cr SIP daala". Or contrast pharma: "Nifty 1 saal mein -11.8%, Nifty Pharma upar".
