# Pharma fact-check: semaglutide / Natco / Zydus
Checked 2026-10-27. Read-only. Status is measured against the brief.

## Headline
"Upcoming March semaglutide patent expiration" is **WRONG as of today**. The Indian patent ran out on **20 March 2026**, and generics went on sale from **21 March 2026**, seven months ago. The catalyst has already played out. Natco and Zydus are two of about 10 launchers, not the only ones.

## What this means for the catalyst section
1. Drop "upcoming March expiry". It happened on 20–21 Mar 2026. Present it as a catalyst that has already played out, and use what came next (price war, market share, plateau) as the story.
2. Natco and Zydus are two of about 10 launchers. Zydus has the stronger angle (obesity approval, exclusive reusable pen, Lupin and Torrent licensing, #2 generic share in Apr). Natco's angle is the cheapest vial, but it has a diabetes-only label and isn't in the Apr top-6 share list.
3. Pharmarack says semaglutide value plateaued at about Rs 64 cr/month by Jun 2026. Any forward catalyst now has to be export markets (Canada, Brazil, China) or obesity labels, and neither company has a sourced overseas launch. Keep it hedged.
