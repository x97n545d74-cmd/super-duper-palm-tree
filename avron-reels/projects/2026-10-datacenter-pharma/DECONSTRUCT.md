## Hard questions
- **Predictable:** "two sectors immune to crashes" is a claim every finfluencer makes, and it's false (no sector is immune). It also invites the sceptical-investor swipe.
- **Generic:** "secular growth", "structural shift" said without a number.
- **Overcomplicated:** six data-centre names in 20 s plus two pharma names. Eight logos in 35 s is a slideshow.
- **Where viewers leave:** 0:12–0:25 (abstract "two sectors" talk with no number), and the value-chain list if each name gets no reason.
- **Deserves screen time:** the value-chain map (land → compute → cooling) and the dated semaglutide event.
- **Needs checking:** recent crash/dip, 95% capacity, every company's data-centre link (Jyoti CNC and Voltas look weakest), semaglutide expiry date, Natco/Zydus launches, "giants".
- **Angle competitors miss:** the crash you were waiting for isn't the only way in. Themes have layers, and the layer you buy changes the risk. Picks-and-shovels framing, told with what each company itself says.

## Story fix before sentences
Brief's order: insult → "immune sectors" → name list → catalyst → picks CTA.
New order: recognition hook → the dip that actually happened (number) → one theme shown as a chain, three layers, each with one company fact → second theme as a dated event (semaglutide) with the competition reality → the lesson (pick the layer, and no sector is crash-proof) → CTA question about layers.
