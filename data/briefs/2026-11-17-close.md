## Market regime
**Weak.** Most stocks are falling. Breakouts fail more often than not, and protecting capital comes first.

Breadth is flat: 827 advancers against 833 decliners, 45.7% of stocks above the 200-day average, and 25 new 52-week highs against 2 lows. The average liquid stock is down 3.7% over 20 days, and only 30% of them rose. The index gains are narrow. Nifty Bank rose 1.13% to 62,273.25 and Nifty 50 rose 0.38%, while Smallcap 250 fell 0.09% and Nifty IT fell 0.9%. India VIX is 15.52. Over a month the strongest industries were Media (+6.1%), Realty (+6.0%) and Healthcare (+5.9%). The weakest were Utilities (-10.6%), Capital Goods (-6.2%) and IT (-6.0%).

Foreign investors bought a net Rs 5,108 crore of shares. They are still net short index futures, holding only 33.4% long, and the index put-call ratio is 1.15. That describes positioning, not direction.

## What changed in businesses
**Libas (LIBAS):** The auditor's qualified review says ₹700.84 lakhs of inventory is obsolete and profit is overstated by the same amount. That is more than the half-year standalone profit of Rs 142.99 lakhs. Read: the reported profit may be a loss. Receivables are about 355 days of sales, and the filing came after about 18:10 IST, so the 11.22 close does not reflect it. Risk: the results code also flags a profit and EPS mismatch.

**Richa Info Systems:** H1 revenue fell to ₹3,551.35 lakh from ₹8,593.06 lakh. Operating cash flow was ₹(1,401.58) lakh, and receivables are above 300 days. Risks: repeated dilution, and the filing has template text and internal inconsistencies.

**ION Exchange:** CRISIL cut the outlook to Negative and kept A+/A1. H1 PAT was Rs -80 crore after a ~Rs 90 crore legacy-project provision. Receivables are 142 days. The stock is in stage 4, and the fall was on 0.6x volume.

**Chandan Healthcare:** Q2 PAT of ₹18.61 Cr includes a ₹10.23 Cr one-off gain from selling a unit to a group company. Without it, Q2 PAT is about ₹8.38 Cr (the filing's own figures). Risk: the sale price had no market test.

**International Conveyors:** Q2 revenue rose 81.9% and EBITDA margin was 28.0%. PAT was still a ₹0.7 Cr loss on treasury mark-to-market. EBITDA is the cleaner guide, and Q2 may be lumpy.

**Paramount Dye Tec:** Sales rose but PAT fell (Rs 138.05 lakh against Rs 216.47 lakh). The auditor's emphasis of matter says inventory and receivables are unverified.

**Results (ICELCO):** Revenue rose 36.1% and PAT 124.7%, with EBITDA margin up 7.1 points. Operating cash flow is under half of profit, so check that.

**India Glycols:** The year-on-year drop is a demerger scope change. Q2 against Q1 is flat on revenue, with EBITDA of Rs. 39.34 Cr against Rs. 40.21 Cr.

**BGR Energy:** NCLAT directed that the company be marked "Active", but the interim stay continues and the matter returns in two weeks. The company has not seen the order text.

**Orders and deals:** Ashoka Buildcon is L1 on a Rs.1,465 Cr NHAI package. It is not yet an award, and the stock is in a stage 4-type chart. Bajaj Finance's QIP is about 1.83% dilution (the filing's share numbers, my arithmetic). Signature's consortium bid Rs.194.19 Cr for Raigad land.

**Themes:** Renewables and power is rising, with Acme Solar's 220 MW PPA and Advait's ₹130.49 crore GETCO order.

**Government:** The RBI announced a ₹50,000 crore overnight VRRR auction for 18 November (official, liquidity drain). From news, not official, Krishca's subsidiary Vajra Alloys was approved under the specialty steel PLI scheme.
