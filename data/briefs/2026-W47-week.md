## The week in one paragraph
A narrow, bank-led, foreign-flow rally, not a broad one. Nifty 50 closed at 25,800.60, up 2.61% for the week. Nifty Bank hit a record 64,635.20 (+2.49% on Friday). Foreign investors bought a net ₹7,306.4 crore of cash equities on the latest day. Underneath, 1,034 stocks fell against 641 that rose, only 45.1% sit above their 200-day average, and Smallcap 250 (-0.1%) and Microcap 250 (-0.87%) were weaker than the large indices. The regime label is "weak"; the average liquid stock fell 3.3% over 20 days. India VIX rose 7.51% to 17.32, and FIIs are still net short index futures (34.1% long). The week's main lesson is that a headline index gain was not a signal about the typical small cap.

## What the screens taught us
Record: 19 sample days to Nov 13. The average stock returned -0.52% over 5 days and -3.32% over 20.
- **Beat the average:** oversold-in-uptrend (+1.78 and +4.38 points over 5 and 20 days), overbought (+0.34, +1.42) and delivery spike (+0.48, +0.75).
- **Lagged:** golden cross (-0.85, -1.43), trend template (-0.22, -1.04), and 20-day breakouts at 20 days (-0.27).
- **Read:** in a weak tape, dips in uptrends have been bought and leaders have kept running. Cross-over and template signals have been late. This week's overbought and 52-week-breakout lists are full of bank ETFs, PSU banks and DLF, so they mostly reflect the bank rally, not breadth. Four stocks (UTSSAV, MODISONLTD, SOTL, GKSL) are on the oversold-in-uptrend list. Samples are small, so treat the edges as tendencies.
