## What changed in businesses
**SML Mahindra (rights issue).** Fact: up to ₹1,449.65 cr at ₹3,506 per share, 2 for 7. About 2.6 times net worth, with ₹525 cr going to buy M&M's truck-bus unit, which had negative EBIT in all three years shown. Read: pro rata pricing does not shift value to the promoter. The real question is what minorities pay for, and how much flows back to M&M. H1 profit fell while revenue rose. Risk: heavy dilution and a related-party dependence. Ex-date is Nov 20.

**Videocon Industries and Value Industries.** Fact: the Supreme Court dismissed Twinstar's appeals, so the ₹2,962.02 cr plan stays set aside and the matter returns to the CoC. Admitted claims are ₹64,838.63 cr. Read: nothing in the filing points to value for equity holders. Risk: years of delay, with no bidder or timeline named.

**United Drilling Tools.** Fact: Q2 revenue ₹1,752.80 lakh against ₹5,560.18 lakh a year ago, still with PAT of ₹170.37 lakh. H1 operating cash flow was negative. Read: the company calls its quarters lumpy, but cash is the worry. Receivables are 268 days of sales, and the stock is in stage 4. Risk: an uncapped subsidiary guarantee.

**Drone Destination.** Fact: H1 operating cash flow of -₹61,890.75 thousand against PAT of ₹14,553.97 thousand. Other income is 53.4% of pre-tax profit, and receivables are 393 days of sales. Read: profit without cash, funded by short-term debt. Risk: very thin trading.
