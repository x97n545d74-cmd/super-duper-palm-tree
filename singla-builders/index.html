About Singla Builders

Engineered integrity. Reliable handover.

Over 20+ years of active field execution, we treat every milestone as an exact science.

Singla Builders is a Mohali-based construction company run by a working civil engineer, not a middleman. The person who prices your project is the same person who checks the reinforcement before a pour.

That is how we avoid the usual contractor bottlenecks: every job runs from proper engineering drawings, materials are tested and certified before they go into the structure, and the schedule is monitored milestone by milestone.

How a project runs

Six stages. No surprises.

Every job is scheduled with the critical path method (CPM), so you always know what is happening on site this week and what comes next.

01

Site visit & brief

We meet on your plot, understand the requirement and check site conditions.

02

Estimate & BOQ

An itemised estimate, so the budget is clear before any commitment.

03

Who runs Singla Builders?

Singla Builders is led by Er. Rajesh Kumar, a B.Tech Civil Engineering graduate of Guru Nanak Dev Engineering College (GNDEC), Ludhiana, with more than 20 years of on-site construction experience. He is the principal contractor on every project.

Do you build residential houses and kothis?

Yes. Alongside commercial and infrastructure work, we take turnkey contracts for independent houses, kothis, villas and flats, using tested concrete, Fe550 steel and water-tight basement construction.

How do I get a construction cost estimate?

Share your plot size, location and requirements through the form below, on WhatsApp, or by phone. Our estimation team responds within 24 hours. Every estimate is itemised, so you can see what you are paying for.

Which areas do you work in?

We are headquartered in Mohali and work across the Chandigarh tricity (Mohali, Chandigarh, Zirakpur, Kharar, Panchkula, New Chandigarh) and Punjab. Commercial packages for corporate clients are taken up elsewhere in India.

Do you also deal in real estate?

Yes. Besides construction and engineering consultancy, we assist clients with the sale and purchase of property, and can advise on what it will take to build on a plot before you buy it.

Can you price a tender or RFP from our drawings?

Yes. Send the scope of work, drawings if available, land area and location using the enquiry form or WhatsApp, and we will come back with a priced proposal.
